01 · Approach

How I think about this work.

My job is to help you think clearly about your money — what you have, what you'll need, and the decisions in front of you. That usually means slowing the conversation down, not speeding it up. Pension elections, rollover decisions, and Social Security claiming are the kind of choices you make once. They deserve careful, considered attention.

I don't predict markets. I don't promise outcomes. I help build a plan that's reasonable across a range of futures, then revisit it when life changes.

I'm independent — meaning I'm not pushed to recommend any particular product or strategy. I work for the people who hire me, and I try to stay worth the work.
