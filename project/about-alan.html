01 · Philosophy

Personal relationships, taken seriously.

Working in financial services has taught me the importance of developing personal relationships with clients. They are entrusting me with their life savings. I take that responsibility seriously and do my best to always act with integrity, communicate effectively, and treat everyone with the utmost respect.
02 · How I got here

A career that turned personal.

A little over ten years ago, my father's health began to decline and I started helping him with his finances. At that time, I had been working as a Finance Manager at a mutual fund company for fifteen years and held a Bachelor's and Master's in Finance — so I assumed the work would be straightforward. It wasn't.

To help my father, I had to research the unique rules around Medicare options, 401(k) distributions, pensions, Social Security, and how taxes affect each of them. The research took far more time than I expected. Working through it, I came to a conclusion: if it took me — with a finance background — this much effort, what about someone without one?

That's when I decided to help others prepare for, and enjoy, their retirement. I now have over 25 years in the Financial Services industry, and currently hold Series 6, 63, and 66 licenses along with a Texas Department of Insurance Life & Health license. Let me put that experience to work for you.
