01 / About Alan

An advisor who treats your life savings as
a personal responsibility.

"Clients are entrusting me with their life savings. I take that responsibility seriously — acting with integrity, communicating clearly, and treating everyone with the utmost respect."

My journey to becoming a Financial Advisor began a little over ten years ago, when my father's health declined and I began helping him with his finances. At the time, I had spent fifteen years as a Finance Manager at a mutual fund company — with a B.S. and M.S. in Finance — so I expected the work to be straightforward. It wasn't.

Researching Medicare options, 401(k) distributions, pensions, Social Security, and how taxes interact with each took far more time than I expected. If it took me that long, I thought, what about someone without a finance background? That's when I decided to help others prepare for — and enjoy — their retirement.

I've now been in the Financial Services industry for over 25 years. I currently hold Series 6, 63, and 66 licenses, plus a Texas Department of Insurance Life & Health license. Let me put that experience to work for you.

04 / How We Work Together

A four-step process,
built for the long term.

Most relationships start with a conversation, not paperwork. The first meeting is a chance to understand your situation and decide together whether working together makes sense.
Step 01

Discover

A no-obligation fact-finding conversation. We talk about what you have, what you owe, what concerns you, and what you'd like the next chapter to look like.

~ 45 min · in person or virtual
Step 02

Plan & Design

I review the documents, model scenarios, and develop a written set of recommendations sized to your goals, risk capacity, and timeline.

~ 2–3 weeks
Step 03

Implement

Paperwork handled. Accounts opened or transferred. Beneficiaries reviewed. Insurance and other moving parts coordinated with your CPA and attorney as needed.

~ 30 days, typical
Step 04

Monitor

Regular reviews and life-event check-ins. Markets move; lives shift; the plan gets updated. The goal is a plan that stays current — not one that ages on a shelf.

Ongoing · annually at minimum
